FAQs

What are your rental requirements?

We require a valid ID, proof of income, and a security deposit equal to one month's rent. Some properties may have additional requirements.

How long does the approval process take?

Typically 1-2 business days after we receive all required documents. We'll notify you immediately once approved.

Do you offer short-term rentals?

Yes, we offer flexible rental terms starting from 1 month. Rates may vary based on rental duration.
