The story follows a protagonist who discovers a supernatural connection to rainfall. While ordinary under a clear sky, the arrival of rain unlocks unparalleled gifts:
• 💥 Superhuman Power: Physical strength and energy that scales with the intensity of the storm.
• 🧠 Hyper-Intelligence: An unlocked mind capable of advanced strategic thinking and complex problem-solving while it rains.
• 🎲 Absolute Luck: Uncanny, reality-bending good fortune that turns any rainy situation in his favor.
• 🔮 Meteorological Precongnition: The unique ability to perfectly predict exactly when, where, and how hard it will rain.
