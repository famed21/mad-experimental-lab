# S-Ranks-Lab 🧠🏆

**Lesotho's National Intelligence Competition**  
*Test. Think. Solve. Rise.*

## Overview

S-Ranks-Lab is an online competitive intelligence and problem-solving platform designed to discover, challenge, and recognize exceptional minds across Lesotho.

Participants create accounts, solve challenging problems online, earn points, improve their competitive ratings, and compete for positions on the national leaderboard. The competition aims to identify individuals who demonstrate outstanding logical reasoning, mathematical ability, abstract thinking, strategic intelligence, and problem-solving skills.

## Competition Categories

- **Abstract Reasoning** — Pattern recognition, spatial reasoning, and non-verbal logic.
- **Mathematics** — Numerical reasoning, mathematical logic, and challenging mathematical problems.
- **Logical Reasoning** — Deduction, analytical thinking, and multi-step reasoning.
- **Computational Thinking** — Algorithms, structured problem-solving, and computational puzzles.
- **Strategic Thinking** — Decision-making, planning, and reasoning under constraints.
- **Complex Problem-Solving** — Difficult, original challenges requiring creativity and analytical ability.

## Competitive Ranking System

Participants earn points by completing eligible challenges and develop a competitive rating based on their performance. National and category leaderboards showcase top performers, while achievements and rank progression recognize continued excellence.

## Top 5 Finalists

The five highest-ranked eligible finalists will receive:

- Prizes and recognition for their achievements.
- Certificates of excellence.
- Internship opportunities at **Famed Labs — Applied Intelligence**, where selected finalists can gain practical experience, collaborate on technical projects, and develop their problem-solving abilities.

*Final eligibility requirements, prize details, internship terms, and competition rules will be published before the official competition begins.*

## Platform Features

- Open competitor registration and secure authentication.
- Personal dashboards and competitor profiles.
- Timed online challenges and automated scoring.
- Points, competitive ratings, and rank progression.
- National, category, and seasonal leaderboards.
- Challenge management and administration dashboard.
- Competition integrity controls and score appeals.
- Verified results and recognition of top performers.

## Vision

To establish a credible national platform that celebrates intellectual ability, encourages analytical thinking, and helps exceptional talent in Lesotho access opportunities to learn, innovate, and contribute to technological development.
